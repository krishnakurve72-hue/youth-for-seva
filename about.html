Founded in Bengaluru, built for the whole country

Youth for Seva began in April 2007 when Sri Venkatesh Murthy set out to give India's young people a structured, meaningful way to volunteer. What started as a local effort has grown into one of the country's largest organised volunteering movements.

Every programme is built around two ideas:

Runa

The debt we owe to the society that raised us.

Yajna

The spirit of selfless giving.
