Service is a way of life — not an event.

Youth for Seva is a nationwide volunteering movement founded in 2007, channeling the energy of young India into education, health, livelihood and rural development work.

Become a volunteer See our programs

Founded in Bengaluru, built for the whole country

Youth for Seva began in April 2007 when Sri Venkatesh Murthy set out to give India's young people a structured, meaningful way to volunteer. What started as a local effort has grown into one of the country's largest organised volunteering movements.

Every programme is built around two ideas: Runa, the debt we owe to the society that raised us, and Yajna, the spirit of selfless giving.

Where the work happens

Vidya Chetana

Scholarships for students from underprivileged backgrounds pursuing PUC, degree, engineering and medical studies.

Doctors for Seva

Medical professionals offering voluntary care: school health check-ups, adolescent health awareness and community camps.

Seva Circle

Community volunteering groups active in 15 cities, open to anyone aged 16 and above.

Environment & Rural Development

Plantation drives and integrated rural development work building self-reliant communities.
